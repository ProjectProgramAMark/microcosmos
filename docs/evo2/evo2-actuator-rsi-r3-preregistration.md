## 1. Purpose of r3

r2 fixed the invalid founder prerequisite and then completed the full frozen
actuator grid. Its result was scientifically valid but negative: injury gain
`0.1` reliably harmed clone, reproduction remained active, but none of the
three always-on mutation policies beat clone across all training founders.

That is not a reason to weaken the injury or select a favorable repeat. It
reveals that the old positive-control gate was logically misaligned with the
research question. Always mutating every birth creates mutation load. The
target Shinka program instead chooses among clone, parametric, structural, and
mixed heredity per birth from ecological state. Requiring an always-on policy
to solve the task before searching for an adaptive scheduler assumes away the
thing the outer loop is meant to discover.

r3 therefore treats r2 as immutable benchmark qualification and proceeds to a
prospective matched outer search. No r2 result is relabeled as confirmatory.
Development and sealed founders, seeds, injury locations, manifests, and scores
have not been opened or evaluated.

genome. Different worlds use different founder genotypes. Clonality ensures
that useful post-injury genetic novelty must be generated by heredity rather
than selected from standing genetic variation.

differ only at the frozen event boundary:
